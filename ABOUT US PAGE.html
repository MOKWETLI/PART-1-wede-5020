HISTORY OF ORGANASATION

Mokwetli glazier pty was proposed by Lehlogonolo and Thomas Mokwetli on 2019.The business was introduced due to lack of employment in our society to create jobs.

Company first branch was built on 2020 at Mokopane town at Limpopo province.Three years mokwetli pty had five branches which were located at Polokwane,Lephalale,Siyabuswa,Musina and main branch was at Mokopane town which all branches are located at limpopo.Company faced first balance sheet of R234 867,67c liabilities which lead to change of ownership and management.The new manager became Kgotsofatso Kekana.
